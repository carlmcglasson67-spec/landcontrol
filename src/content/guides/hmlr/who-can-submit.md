## The rule

Practice guide 84 section 6.2 says the conveyancer who provides the information must be an **individual who is personally regulated**: for example, an individually regulated solicitor, licensed conveyancer or chartered legal executive. Working for a regulated firm is not enough. Employees of regulated firms qualify only if they are personally regulated.

The conveyancer gives their professional registration number and the authority that issued it. That is not published.

## Responsibility stays with the grantee

Section 6.1 keeps the duty with the grantee, who is accountable for material errors or omissions. Using a conveyancer does not transfer the legal responsibility. The grantee needs to:

- give the conveyancer complete, accurate information;
- instruct them in time to meet each deadline; and
- get confirmation that the submission has been made.

## What it means for each type of organisation

**National housebuilders with in-house legal teams:** possible to file in-house, provided the individuals submitting are personally regulated and have Portal access.

**Regional developers and promoters:** expect to instruct external conveyancers for every grant, assignment, variation and ending. Agree a fixed process and fee before April 2027.

**Land promoters with small teams:** the same, with extra care over fallback rights inside promotion agreements, each of which may need its own submission.

**Registered social landlords:** golden brick deals may make the RSL the grantee (HMLR scenario 7). Check who in your legal function is individually regulated.

## Capacity is the real risk

Every in-scope right signed from 8 June 2026 to 5 April 2027 is due before 6 October 2027, and only individually regulated people can file. The conveyancers who handle strategic land are a small pool. Firms that book capacity and have data ready will file in the first weeks; others will be queuing in September 2027.

## How to set it up

1. Name the submitting conveyancer for each right in your register.
2. Agree a standard instruction pack: the [information HMLR asks for](/hmlr/information-required/) plus the spatial file.
3. Agree turnaround times against each 60-day deadline.
4. Decide how the conveyancer confirms filing, and where you keep the evidence.
