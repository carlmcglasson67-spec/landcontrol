## Why conditional contracts are caught

The regime is about control short of ownership. A buyer under a conditional contract does not own the land yet, but nobody else can buy it while the contract stands, and the buyer decides whether to push for the conditions to be satisfied. That is exactly the kind of control the government wants to see. The MHCLG guidance confirms that conditional rights are covered, including those conditional on planning permission.

The usual tests apply: a written contract over registered land, granted by the registered owner, for a transfer of the freehold or a lease of 15 years or more, held for the purposes of an undertaking, with no exemption.

## The short-term exemption still bites

Many conditional contracts have a long-stop date. If, counting every extension, the long-stop could fall 18 months or more after the contract date, the short-term exemption is lost. A contract that can only last less than 18 months is exempt.

## What HMLR will ask for

The information is the same as for any contractual control right (see [what HMLR will ask for](/hmlr/information-required/)), but the fields that need most care for a conditional contract are:

- **When the right can be exercised, or the conditions:** describe the planning condition and any other conditions precedent.
- **The initial period of control:** from the contract date to the latest date on which completion could happen under the contract, ignoring extensions.
- **Extensions:** long-stop extensions, appeal periods, automatic rollovers.
- **Termination:** either party’s right to rescind if conditions are not met.

## The end of the right

For a conditional contract, Practice guide 84 treats the right as exercised when the disposition the contract requires is made. If the buyer completes, the grantee reports the ending within 60 days. If the contract is rescinded because planning is refused, that is a determination, and it too is reported within 60 days, saying whether the right ended wholly or in part.

## Deadlines at a glance

- Contract exchanged **8 June 2026 to 5 April 2027**: report before **6 October 2027**.
- Exchanged **on or after 6 April 2027**: within **60 days** of exchange.
- Varied in writing, assigned or ended from 6 April 2027: within **60 days** of that event.
