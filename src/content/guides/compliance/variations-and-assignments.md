## Why this matters more than new grants

New grants are a flow. The stock of existing options, conditional contracts and promotion agreements is far bigger, and most of it was signed before 8 June 2026. None of that stock has to be reported just because the regime starts. But Practice guide 84 is clear that rights granted before 8 June 2026 become reportable when they are assigned or varied on or after 6 April 2027.

So the first time an older option is extended, assigned within a group, or sold to another developer after April 2027, it lands on the register.

## What counts as a variation

A variation is a trigger only if both are true:

1. it is **in writing**; and
2. it **alters information already required**: the type or scope of the right, the land affected, the initial period of control, extension or termination provisions, or the conditions for exercise.

Extending the term of an option, adding or removing land, changing the long-stop in a conditional contract, or adding a termination right all qualify. A change to the price formula alone probably does not, because price is not part of the contractual control information. Write down the reasoning when you decide a variation is not a trigger.

## What counts as an assignment

An assignment moves the benefit of the right to a **new grantee**. Practice guide 84 treats it as a trigger, and HMLR’s scenario 2 shows how it can bring a right into scope for the first time: an option held by an individual for his family home is not reportable, but once assigned to a builder it is held for an undertaking and the builder must report it.

Watch for:

- intra-group transfers on reorganisations;
- sales of a strategic land portfolio to another developer;
- promoters assigning the benefit to a housebuilder at the end of a promotion.

## The drift into the short-term exemption

There is one escape route. Practice guide 84 gives the example of an option granted in 2025 and assigned in 2028 that may not need reporting if, by then, it has become short-term, meaning it can last less than 18 months from that point with no extensions. Check the remaining term every time.

## Overlapping changes

Where an assignment and a variation happen close together, one submission can cover both if it is updated before the deadline. If the right ends within 60 days of the change, you can confirm the ending instead. Calculate each clock with the [deadline calculator](/deadline-calculator/).
