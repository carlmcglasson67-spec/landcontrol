## Three groups of agreements

| Granted | Position |
|---|---|
| Before 8 June 2026 | Not reportable on grant. Brought in by an assignment or reportable variation on or after 6 April 2027, or a notice or restriction application |
| 8 June 2026 to 5 April 2027 | Transitional. Report from 6 April 2027 and before 6 October 2027 |
| On or after 6 April 2027 | Standard rule. Report within 60 days of grant |

Not sure which group an agreement is in? The [transitional period checker](/transitional-period-checker/) works it out.

## How the transitional rule works

Practice guide 84 section 4.2 says the grantee of a right granted on or after 8 June 2026 but before 6 April 2027 must provide the information **before 6 October 2027**. You cannot do it early: the digital service only opens on 6 April 2027. That leaves six months to clear every in-scope deal signed over ten months.

Two consequences follow:

- **Only the grant is a trigger in this period.** An assignment or variation between 8 June 2026 and 5 April 2027 does not start its own clock.
- **But the information must be current.** HMLR’s scenario 9 is a right granted after 8 June 2026 and varied in January 2027 to extend it to four years in total. What the grantee submits must reflect the variation and be accurate at the date the information is provided.

## When October comes early

Until 6 April 2027 you can protect a transitional right by notice or restriction in the usual way. After that, HMLR will generally not accept a notice or restriction application until the information is in. So if you need to protect a transitional right between April and October 2027, you report first, and your real deadline is the day you apply. A written variation or assignment on or after 6 April 2027 also starts its own 60-day clock, which can land before October.

## Older agreements

Agreements signed before 8 June 2026 are not swept in automatically. They are caught the first time they change in a way that matters from 6 April 2027: an assignment, or a written variation that changes the reportable information. Practice guide 84 notes that by then a right may have become short-term (less than 18 months left), in which case it may not need reporting at all.

## What to do now

1. Pull a list of every option, conditional contract, pre-emption and promotion agreement signed on or after 8 June 2026.
2. Log every variation and assignment since signing: you will need the current position.
3. Gather the information and spatial files now, so filing in April 2027 is a data-entry job.
4. Book the conveyancer capacity you will need.
