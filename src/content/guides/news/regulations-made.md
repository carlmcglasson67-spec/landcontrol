## What the Regulations do

They require the grantee of a contractual control right over registered land in England and Wales to provide prescribed information to HM Land Registry, through a regulated conveyancer, within set periods. HMLR will publish most of that information as a dataset. Four kinds of right are covered: options, conditional contracts, pre-emption rights and rights to direct or request a sale or lease. See [the complete guide](/contractual-control-agreements/).

## Why 8 June 2026 matters

The Regulations catch rights granted from the day they were made, not the day they come into force. Anything signed from 8 June 2026 to 5 April 2027 has to be reported once the service opens, and before 6 October 2027. Land teams that carried on signing over the summer of 2026 on the assumption that the regime starts in April 2027 already have a backlog.
