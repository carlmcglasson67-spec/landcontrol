## What was published

On 26 October 2026 HM Land Registry published two documents:

- **[Practice guide 84: contractual control rights](/hmlr/practice-guide-84/)**, the operational guide for conveyancers on the Provision of Information (Contractual Control) (Registered Land) Regulations 2026 (SI 2026/615); and
- **[Contractual control rights scenarios](/hmlr/contractual-control-scenarios/)**, nine worked examples of how HMLR currently reads the Regulations.

HMLR said reading the guide now would help conveyancers identify affected transactions and advise clients early, and that it will continue to provide updates ahead of the digital service going live.

## What is new

- **The service has a name.** “Report contractual control over land” will launch in the HMLR Portal from 6 April 2027, with Business Gateway to follow.
- **A floorspace test.** The non-development exemption is tied to housing or development creating 100 square metres or more of floorspace.
- **Secondary rights.** Generally only the primary right is covered; a secondary right is covered if protected by a notice or restriction.
- **Notice applications as a trigger.** An application for a notice or restriction requires the information first, whenever the right was granted.
- **How rights end.** When “exercised” happens for each type of right, and confirmation within 60 days.
- **Reasonable excuse.** Following the guidance and reasonably concluding information is not required may amount to a reasonable excuse.

## What it confirms

The 60-day clocks; the 6 October 2027 deadline for rights granted from 8 June 2026 to 5 April 2027; the individually regulated conveyancer rule; Shapefile or GeoJSON in British National Grid for part-title rights; and publication of the dataset from April 2028.

## What is still to come

Technical guidance for the digital service, the exact questions, fees, any correction process, and day-counting and spatial tolerance rules. All are on the [implementation tracker](/tracker/).

If you are a grantee, start the list of rights granted since 8 June 2026 now. The [transitional period checker](/transitional-period-checker/) and [readiness checklist](/readiness-checklist/) will get you most of the way.
